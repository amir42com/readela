## What was changed

`src/ui/popup.html`, `popup.css` and `popup.js`.

- **The total** stands at the end of the line the bookmark's message begins,
  as secondary information: "A bookmark is saved in this conversation." and,
  at the other end, "12 total". It is still the number of conversations
  that have a bookmark, on both sites, and still named so to a screen
  reader ("12 bookmarked conversations in total").
- **The shortcuts** are a part of their own under that line: the name
  Shortcuts, and beside it the control that opens the browser's settings
  (Set shortcuts, or Change shortcuts). Where keys are assigned, each is a
  pair in two columns, the action in the first and its key in the second,
  as a list of terms and not as a sentence put together with spaces. A key
  is shown exactly as the browser reports it. Where none is assigned the
  name is followed by "Not set" on the same line. Where the browser reports
  nothing about its commands, the part is not shown at all.
- **No word of a choice can break.** The choices of a row share its width
  evenly, and none can be narrower than its word. Nothing asks the browser
  to break a word any more.
- **The rows are sized from the width the content really has.** The room at
  the sides and the column of the names are 14 and 82 pixels in the popup's
  own 360, and go down to 12 and 76 in 345 and less. A rule written for the
  popup's width does not see a scrollbar: with Help open the popup is still
  360 pixels wide to such a rule, while its content has 345.
- **Enlarged text and very little room.** Where the words of a row do not
  fit beside one another, choices go on to a second line whole; four of
  them then stand two by two. The text is no longer made smaller in a
  narrow room.

The popup is not wider: 360 pixels. The rules for little room are still
kept from rooms narrower than 120 pixels, the narrow room Chrome starts
sizing a popup in.

## Measured

### The popup as Chrome opens it from the toolbar

The installed extension, opened with `action.openPopup`, in a visible
Chrome window with a fresh temporary profile, and the same without a
window.

| | Before (`b190ac4`) | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Help closed | 360 × 469, nothing to scroll | 360 × 469, nothing to scroll |
| Help open, the window | 360 × 600, scrolling | 360 × 600, scrolling |
| Help open, the content beside the scrollbar | 345 wide | 345 wide |
| Help open, a row of four choices | 54.7 pixels each; "Original" on two lines in two rows, those choices 48 pixels tall | 57.5 pixels each; every choice on one line, 32 pixels tall |

Without a window Chrome takes no room from the content for a scrollbar, so
a word cannot break there for that reason; it is the run in a window that
shows the fault and its correction. The end-to-end suite
therefore also lays the popup out in 345 and in 343 pixels with Help open,
which is the room a scrollbar leaves.

### The bookmark's part

The built popup files rendered by each browser's engine against a stand-in
for the extension; Chrome 154 and Firefox 157 agree to the pixel.

| State | Before | After |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| No shortcut assigned | 360 × 469 | 360 × 469 |
| Three shortcuts assigned | 360 × 517 | 360 × 522 |
| One shortcut assigned | — | 360 × 486 |
| The browser reports nothing about shortcuts | 360 × 469 | 360 × 442 |
| At the limit of 1000, three shortcuts, a change of settings not stored | 360 × 540 | 360 × 561 |

With three shortcuts every action begins 14 pixels from the edge and every
key 135.8 pixels from it, each pair on one line. The popup is five pixels
taller than before with three keys (a name of its own for the part), and
21 taller in the fullest state, where the long message now shares its line
with the total and takes a third line; both are within what a browser
gives a popup.

### Less room and enlarged text

| State | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Help open in 345 and in 343 pixels | "Original" broken where it is the selected choice | four choices in a row, none broken |
| A room of 320 pixels | four in a row, the text of the choices at 12 pixels | four in a row, the text at 13 pixels as everywhere |
| A room of 280 pixels | a name over its choices, two by two | a name over its choices, four in a row |
| Text a fifth larger | "Original" broken as "Origin" and "al" | four in a row, none broken; 494 pixels tall |
| Text half as large again | — | four choices two by two, none broken |

In none of the twenty-six states rendered in each browser is an element
wider than its box or past the popup's edge, a word of a choice broken, or
a text below 4.5 to 1 against its ground.

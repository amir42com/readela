## The popup

### What was wrong

**Measured**, the popup of `9a76f6b` as Chrome 154 and Firefox 157 lay it
out, the same in both: 559 pixels tall on a conversation with the settings
as installed, 598 on a tab that is not a conversation, 599 with Night and
Readela Sans chosen, 621 after "Bookmark saved.", and up to 675 with its
longest messages. A browser gives a popup 600 pixels of height at most, at a
zoom of 110% that is 545 of the page's own pixels, and a small screen gives
less. Opened by Chrome as a real popup in a session whose screen is 600
pixels high, it was given 510 pixels for its 559: it scrolled, and the
footer was out of view.

### What was changed

Layout and spacing only; no control was added, removed, hidden or made
smaller (a row of choices is 32 pixels tall and a button 30, as before).

- The popup is 360 pixels wide instead of 320.
- The one line a group says about its selected choice ("Warm, dark colours
  for responses.") stands beside the group's name instead of on a line of
  its own under the choices. In less room it wraps there.
- The footer is one row: Reset settings at its start, By Amir42 and the
  version at its end. What Reset did is said on a line under it.
- In the Bookmark section the helper line ("Marks your reading position in
  this conversation.") gives way to a message while there is one, and the
  message stands where the helper stood. The section no longer grows by a
  line when something is said, and the line about shortcuts stays in place.
- Gaps between the parts are one to three pixels smaller.

### Measured after

The built popup files rendered by each browser's engine at the popup's own
width; pixels of height. The two browsers agree to the pixel.

| State | Before | After |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| A tab that is not a conversation | 598 | 478 |
| A conversation, settings as installed | 559 | 478 |
| Night, Readela Sans, 125%, 1.75, Auto | 599 | 478 |
| The same, after "Bookmark saved." | 621 | 478 |
| The same, with a two-line message, three shortcuts assigned and what Reset says | 675 | 514 |
| The longest message the bookmark can give | 653 | 510 |
| Readela off | 598 | 495 |

- **Opened by Chrome as a real popup** (`action.openPopup`, the installed
  extension, a session whose screen is 600 pixels high): 360 by 477, nothing
  to scroll, the footer in view. The build before it: given 510 pixels for
  559, scrolling.
- **Zoom.** In its ordinary states the popup fits the 600 pixels a browser
  allows up to a zoom of 125% (478 × 1.25 = 598). Off it fits up to 120%,
  and with the longest messages up to 110%. At 150% it scrolls in every
  state, as before; nothing is cut off.
- **Less room.** Given 320 pixels of width it is 494 to 511 pixels tall in
  its ordinary states and at most 548; the lines beside the group names wrap, no
  element is wider than its box or reaches past the popup's edge, and
  nothing scrolls sideways.
- **Enlarged text.** With every font size a fifth larger and the width
  unchanged it is 555 to 576 pixels tall in its ordinary states, still
  without scrolling, and up to 639 with the longest messages, where it
  scrolls. Nothing is cut off.
- **Seen**: pictures of the rendered popup in both browsers, light and dark,
  with every choice made, with messages, and off. Selected and disabled
  states, the focus ring and the contrast checks are the same code as before
  and pass as before.

## Return when the saved passage is in history the site has not loaded

### What the search does

**Read**, in `src/page/reader.js`, unchanged by this work. When the response
a place was saved in is not in the document, Return moves the conversation's
own scrolling region one stretch at a time towards where the place is
expected and asks again after each move. At the beginning of what is loaded
it waits up to two and a half seconds for the conversation to grow; if it
grows, the walk goes on. A place is accepted only in the response it was
saved in, and is reported as reached only when it is seen in view and is
still there after the page has settled. The search ends at ninety stops or
thirty seconds, when the reader does anything, and when the conversation or
Readela's state changes. Unless it found the place or was taken over, it
puts the conversation back where it was. The bookmark is never removed by a
search.

### What was run

**Verified** on a synthetic long ChatGPT-shaped conversation of forty turns
that opens at its end on its last five, loads the five before them a moment
after its beginning is scrolled into view, keeps only the turns near the
viewport in the document, and ends every reply with the same sentence. The
place is the closing sentence of the seventh turn, thirty turns above what
is loaded when the conversation is opened again. One new end-to-end step,
in Chrome and in Firefox:

| Case | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Six loads are needed and come unevenly, after 150, 1400, 300, 2000, 200 and 900 ms | Each is waited for. "Returned to your bookmark."; the place is marked, in view below the bar, and still there a moment later. No other of the identical sentences is marked. |
| The second load takes five seconds, longer than the search waits for one | "Your bookmark was not found. It is still saved." No reply is marked, nothing is pointed at, the conversation is back at its end, the bookmark is kept. When the load arrives, nothing moves. The next Return goes on from what was loaded and reaches the place. |
| The turn is gone: the site shows the same words under another key | The whole conversation is loaded and looked at, down to its first turn. "Your bookmark was not found. It is still saved." The look-alike is in the document and is not marked; the conversation is back at its end; the bookmark is kept. |

These passed as written the first time they ran, so the search was not
changed. The steps that were there before still pass and cover the rest of
what was asked: a place far above found from the end with even loading; a
site that never loads its earlier part; a site too slow for one search,
which ends at its limit of time (thirty to thirty-one seconds) and gets
there on the next Return; the reader taking over with the wheel, after
which nothing moves the page; a change of conversation and turning Readela
off during a search; a place the site moves after the jump; and the same
sentence in another response never taken for the place.

### What this does not show

It is a synthetic page built after what was read on the real site on
2026-10-10. How long real ChatGPT takes to load earlier turns was not
measured: if a load there regularly takes longer than two and a half
seconds, the first Return says "not found", truthfully, and a second one
continues. The waiting time was not raised without a measurement that calls
for it. The owner's own check is below.

## Not verified

- **The popup on the owner's browser.** Measured and looked at in headless
  Chrome 154 and Firefox 157 on this machine. The owner's zoom and screen
  were not known; the table above says what fits at which zoom.
- **Firefox's own popup window.** There the popup's files were rendered by
  Firefox's engine as a page; the automation cannot open the toolbar popup.
- **Return in unloaded history on a real signed-in conversation.** Not
  attempted: it would need the owner's conversations. Steps for the owner:

  1. Open a long ChatGPT conversation and scroll far up, so that earlier
     messages have loaded. Select a few words in a reply there and press
     Save in the popup.
  2. Open another conversation, then come back to the long one, or reload
     the page. It opens at its latest messages.
  3. Without scrolling, press Return. The page should scroll up by itself,
     pause while earlier messages load, and stop on the saved paragraph,
     marked and outlined for a moment, with "Returned to your bookmark."
  4. If it says "Your bookmark was not found. It is still saved.", the page
     should be back at the latest messages; press Return once more. It must
     never stop on another paragraph and call it the bookmark.
  5. Once more, and scroll with the wheel while it is searching: it should
     stop at once and say "Return was stopped. Your bookmark is still
     saved."

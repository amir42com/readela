## Owner's instruction and the design reference

The owner approved a final design for the popup and asked for it to be
implemented as it is, with these amendments: the rows in the order
Appearance, Direction, Font, Text size, Line spacing, Bookmark; the visible
name Direction, with Text direction kept as the accessible name; Save place
as the bookmark's first action; the sentence "Save your place in this
conversation." in place of a mistyped one in the package; a footer of Reset
settings, By Amir42 and the version and nothing else; what the bookmark has
to say under the bookmark's buttons, and what a change of settings has to
say with the reading settings; the total and the shortcuts as secondary
information under the actions. The typography, how reading settings and
bookmarks work and are stored, permissions, supported sites and the version
number were not to change.

The package (`final-design.rar`, kept outside the repository) holds a
preview page with its pictures in each state, the wording in a table, and
the sizes its author measured: 360 by 475 pixels in its ordinary state. Its
appearance and its wording were used. Its script, which imitates the
extension, was not: the popup keeps its own code and its contracts with the
page and with storage.

## What was changed

`src/ui/popup.html`, `popup.css` and `popup.js`, and one word list in
`src/browser/shortcut.js`.

- **Rows.** Each reading setting is a row: its name at the start, its
  choices at the end. Appearance, Direction, Font, Text size, Line spacing.
  The row a reader sees as Direction is named Text direction to a screen
  reader. The choices are the radio groups they were.
- **A selected choice** has a fill and a border of its own and heavier
  text, at exactly the size of the others. No mark, no underline.
- **The popup wears the chosen appearance.** Paper: the warm light palette.
  Night: the dark one. Original: the browser's own scheme, light or dark.
  It changes as the choice is made, from the one stored setting; there is
  no setting for the popup. Off does not change it.
- **Header and Help.** The name and the switch, no icon. Under them, that
  settings apply at once and what Original means, and Help beside it:
  closed until it is opened, with the seven paragraphs of the package. The
  lines that used to appear under a selected choice are gone; Help says it.
- **Bookmark.** A row like the others: Bookmark, then Save place or Update,
  Return, Remove. The action a reader most likely came for is filled:
  Save place where there is no bookmark, Return where there is one. Under
  the buttons, one line: what holds for this conversation, or what the last
  action did. Under that, smaller: the total, and the keys the browser
  holds with the way to its settings.
- **Messages.** What the bookmark has to say is said under its buttons.
  What a change of settings has to say is said under the settings rows:
  nothing where a change was stored, "Settings reset. Bookmarks kept.", or
  "Could not save your changes." The footer holds Reset settings, By Amir42
  and the version, and no element in which anything could be said.
- **Warnings.** A result that means the action did not do what was asked
  (nothing in view, an ambiguous paragraph, a save or a removal that could
  not be confirmed, the limit of 1000, a bookmark not found, a Return that
  did not finish, a change of settings not stored) is shown with a sign
  before the words and in a colour of its own: the terracotta of the
  bookmark's bar, `#b24a14` on the light palettes and `#f2914a` on the dark
  ones. The sign is drawn, not written, so it is not read out as a word.
- **Wording**, from the package's table, with the owner's corrected
  sentence. At the limit: "All 1000 bookmarks are in use. Open a bookmarked
  conversation and remove its bookmark to save a new one." A failed save is
  "Could not confirm the bookmark was saved.": it is no longer said that
  nothing changed, which the popup cannot know. A failed removal says the
  bookmark is still saved only when the page then reports that it is.

Nothing the popup asks of the page or of storage changed: the same five
requests, the same preferences, the same answers. No state or result was
invented for the new words.

### Where this differs from the package's pictures

- **The total** stands under the actions with the shortcuts, as the
  instruction says, not under the word Bookmark as the pictures show it.
- **Settings messages** stand under the settings rows, as the instruction
  says, not under the footer as the package's table has them.
- **Direction** is one line; the pictures show Text direction on two.

### A fault found in the real popup and corrected

Opened by Chrome as a real popup, the first build of the design was 375
pixels wide for 360 pixels of content: a strip stayed empty beside it.
Chrome sizes a popup to its content starting from a very narrow room; the
layout the popup takes in very little room (a name over its choices) is
tall there, and Chrome left space for a scrollbar that the popup, once
sized, did not need. That layout is now kept for rooms of 120 pixels and
more, which is narrower than any room a popup is given. A step of the
end-to-end suite now opens the popup as Chrome does and holds it to 360
pixels with nothing to scroll.

## Measured

The built popup files rendered by each browser's engine, their script
running against a stand-in for the extension whose answers are set per
state; width by height in the page's own pixels. Chrome 154 and Firefox 157
agree to the pixel in every state.

| State | Size |
| --- | ---: |
| A bookmarked conversation, every choice made, Paper | 360 × 469 |
| The same under Night, and under Original in a light and in a dark browser | 360 × 469 |
| No bookmark; a tab that is not a conversation; Readela off | 360 × 469 |
| After "Bookmark saved.", and after "Your bookmark was not found…" | 360 × 469 |
| After Reset, with its message under the settings | 360 × 491 |
| At the limit of 1000, with three shortcuts assigned and a change of settings not stored | 360 × 540 |
| Help open | 360 × 1009 |
| In a room of 320 pixels, three shortcuts assigned | 320 × 547 |
| In a room of 280 pixels (a name over its choices) | 280 × 759 |
| Every font size a fifth larger, the width unchanged | 360 × 561 |

- **Opened by Chrome as a real popup** (`action.openPopup`, the installed
  extension): 360 by 469, nothing to scroll, the footer in view.
- **Zoom.** A browser gives a popup 600 pixels of height. The ordinary
  states fit up to a zoom of 125% (469 × 1.25 = 586). With the longest
  messages together (540) it fits at 110% and scrolls at 125%. Help open
  scrolls at every zoom, by design.
- **Controls.** A choice and an action are 32 pixels tall, as in the
  package; the choices of a row are one width, the selected one included.
- **Nothing is cut off** in any state: no element is wider than its box or
  reaches past the popup's edge, and nothing scrolls sideways. With the
  text a fifth larger, "Original" breaks within its box in the rows of four
  choices; it is whole and readable.
- **Contrast** of every text against what is behind it, in each of the four
  palettes: 4.5 to 1 or better. The lowest is a warning on Paper, 5.1 to 1.

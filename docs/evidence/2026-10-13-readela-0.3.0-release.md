## Change: the privacy wording

A few sentences said what happens to the conversation text without saying
whose doing it is, and could be read as a statement about the two sites:
"That text is not sent anywhere", "sends nothing anywhere", "nothing leaves
the device". ChatGPT and Claude send every conversation to their own servers;
that is how they work, and Readela has no say in it. The sentences now name
the extension, and the policy, the store description and the README say in
so many words that the two services keep handling conversations under their
own policies.

| Where | Was | Is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `docs/privacy-policy.md`, In short | "It does not send anything anywhere and it does not keep any of the text." | The extension processes the text locally, does not transmit conversation content, uses no analytics or tracking and keeps none of the text; it stores only settings and bookmarks, locally; a bookmark is fingerprints and position numbers. A second paragraph says the policy is about Readela and that the two services handle conversations themselves |
| `docs/privacy-policy.md`, bookmarks | "A fingerprint cannot be turned back into text." | The same, and that someone who can already read the extension's storage on the device could test a guess against it, as `docs/privacy-and-permissions.md` says |
| `docs/store-listing.md`, the Privacy lines of the description | "That text is not sent anywhere … it makes no network request." | Local processing in the browser, in memory; the extension does not transmit conversation content and makes no network request of its own; only preferences and bookmarks are stored, locally; one line on the two services |
| `docs/store-listing.md`, core features, reviewer notes, the note on the certifications, the note on Firefox's data-collection declaration | "no network request", "Nothing is transmitted", "no request leaves the browser", "nothing leaves the device", "stays in the page" | Each names the extension as the one that transmits nothing |
| `README.md`, Privacy and permissions | "sends nothing anywhere" | The wording of the policy, and the paragraph on the two services |
| `docs/privacy-and-permissions.md`, what is stored and sent | "Sent anywhere: nothing." | "Sent by the extension: nothing", and that a site's own traffic is the site's |

No statement of fact about the extension changed, and none had to be made
true by changing it. The checks behind the statements are the ones recorded
in `2026-10-13-readela-0.3.0-release-preparation.md` (no network API and no
dynamic code in what is packaged, `storage.local` with two keys, a bookmark
as hash fingerprints and small numbers, the end-to-end suite refusing every
request a page makes other than for itself and the extension's files); the
packaged files they were made on are the packaged files of this head. The
wording does not call the fingerprints encryption and claims no security
beyond what those checks show.

Nothing that is packaged changed: no file under `src/`, no build script, not
`package.json` or the lockfile. The store images are unchanged; none of them
carries one of the sentences.

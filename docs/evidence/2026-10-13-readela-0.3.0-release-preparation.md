added; the four screenshots of 0.2 were removed. `npm run store-images`
installs `dist/chrome` into a fresh temporary Chrome profile, serves seven
synthetic pages at addresses the extension matches, stores settings under
the key the popup uses, saves three bookmarks with the popup's own button,
and captures pages and popup as the browser renders them.

| `promo-small-440x280.png` | — | — | the same bytes as the file of 0.2.0 (`356c61b2…`); it holds the icon and the name and was not outdated |

- Each screenshot is 1280 by 800, each an opaque 24-bit PNG; the script
  refuses to write one that is not.
- No page asked for anything but itself and the extension's own files.
- The text of the pages is in English in the first, second and fifth, with a
  phrase in Persian and one in Arabic in the second; English, Persian and
  Arabic paragraphs in the third; Persian, Arabic and English in the fourth.
- Around the captures the script draws a frame: a heading, one line, a label
  over each of two captures that are compared, and a last line that says the
  text is an example on a test page. The frame is set in the packaged Inter.
- Legibility at the size a store shows: each image was written at 640 by 400
  and all were put on one sheet. At that size the headings (21 pixels), the
  lines under them (11) and the text of the pages (12 to 13) read; the last
  line (7) does not and is not needed to understand an image. The sheet and
  the reduced copies are kept outside the repository.

What the images are not: they are not pictures of chatgpt.com or claude.ai,
and their text is not a conversation anyone had. `docs/store-listing.md`
says so for whoever uploads them, and the reviewer notes say so to the
stores.

Four of the five screenshots, and the tile, came out the same byte for byte
when the script was run again from the final build. The fifth differed in
five pixels by one level of one colour channel, beside the box a message is
written in; the committed file is the first of the two.

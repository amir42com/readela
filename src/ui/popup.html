Help

Appearance Original leaves response colours unchanged; the popup follows your browser’s light or dark theme. Paper uses warm light colours, and Night uses dark colours, for both responses and the popup.

Text direction Auto chooses text direction from the words in each paragraph. RTL means right-to-left; LTR means left-to-right. Code, mathematics and web addresses keep their own presentation.

Font Readela Sans uses Inter for Latin and Vazirmatn for Persian and Arabic. Other scripts use your device’s fonts.

Text size and line spacing 110%, 125% and 140% enlarge the site’s text size. Line spacing of 1.5, 1.75 or 2.0 sets the line height to that multiple of the text size.

Bookmark One bookmark per conversation, stored on this device. Save place marks the first response paragraph in view, or the paragraph containing your selected text. Update replaces that conversation’s bookmark. Return goes to it; Remove deletes it. The total covers all bookmarked conversations.

Shortcuts Shortcuts are optional and have no keys until you assign them in your browser’s extension shortcut settings. Save place / Update uses the same shortcut.

Reset settings Reset restores Original for appearance, font, text size and line spacing, and Auto for text direction. It keeps Readela On or Off and keeps your bookmarks.
